# Glossary

## Accounts and transactions

**Account type** — every account is one of seven types: **Cash** (bank, e-wallet, physical
cash), **Fixed deposit**, **Investment** (brokerage or crypto exchange), **Retirement**,
**Receivable** (money someone owes you), **Credit** (card, buy-now-pay-later, line of credit)
and **Loan**. The type decides whether the account counts as spendable cash, whether it's part
of your budget, and where it shows in your net worth. See
[Accounts and opening balances](../concepts/accounts-and-opening-balances.md).

**Base currency** — the one currency every total and report is shown in. Accounts can hold any
currency. See [Base currency and FX](../concepts/base-currency-and-fx.md).

**Book** — all of your financial data together: accounts, transactions, budget, holdings and
Lucius's memory. One book lives in one working folder. See
[Where your files live](file-layout.md).

**Cash on hand** — the total of your Cash-type accounts. The same number on every screen. See
[Net worth](../concepts/net-worth.md).

**Cleared** — a transaction you've confirmed against your bank or card statement.

**In budget** — Cash and Credit accounts are part of your budget (they fund or draw on Ready
to Assign); every other type sits outside it. Decided by the account type, not a per-account
switch.

**Kind** — what a transaction is: an expense, income, a transfer between two of your accounts,
a balance adjustment, or an investment buy/sell. See [The ledger](../concepts/the-ledger.md).

**Payee** — who you paid or who paid you. A payee can carry a default category, so its
transactions get categorized automatically.

**Split** — one transaction divided across several categories.

**Starting Balance** — the first transaction in an account: what it held on the day you
started tracking it. See
[Accounts and opening balances](../concepts/accounts-and-opening-balances.md).

**Transfer** — money moving between two of your own accounts. One transaction with both ends,
so it never counts as spending or income.

**Uncategorized** — a transaction with no category. A normal, valid state — nothing breaks if
you leave it. See [Categories](../concepts/categories.md).

## Budgeting (Plan)

**Category** — a label on a transaction ("Groceries"). See
[Categories](../concepts/categories.md).

**Envelope** — a pot of budgeted money in Plan. One or more categories link to it, and their
spending draws it down. See [Envelopes and budgeting](../concepts/envelopes-and-budgeting.md).

**Assigned** — money you've put into an envelope for the month.

**Activity** — what was spent from (or refunded into) an envelope this month.

**Available** — what's left in an envelope right now: carried over from last month, plus
assigned, minus spent. Red means overspent.

**Ready to Assign** — cash in your budget that hasn't been given to an envelope yet. Your goal
is to get it to zero by assigning it.

**Move money** — shift money between two envelopes, or between an envelope and Ready to
Assign.

**Target** — a goal on an envelope: **Set aside** an amount each period, **Fill up to** an
amount, or **Have a balance of** an amount by a date.

**Credit card payment envelope** — an envelope the app creates for each credit card. When you
spend on the card from a funded envelope, that money moves here, ready to pay the bill.

**Commitment** — a dated obligation, like a purchase plan or a loan repayment schedule, with
its own funding envelope. See [Commitments](../features/commitments.md).

## Investing

**Holding** or **position** — how much of one stock, fund, coin or other asset you hold in one
account.

**Lot** — one purchase inside a holding, with its own date, quantity and cost.

**Cost basis** — what you paid for a lot, fees included.

**Realized gain** — profit or loss locked in when you sell. **Unrealized gain** — the
difference between what an unsold holding is worth now and what you paid.

**Settlement cash** — the cash balance inside an investment account. It counts as part of your
investments, not your cash on hand. A negative one means you're borrowing on margin.

**Unvested** — company shares you've been granted but don't own yet. Shown separately and left
out of net worth until they vest. See
[Equity compensation](../features/equity-compensation.md).

**Liquid now / Delayed / Illiquid** — the Dashboard's split of your assets by how soon you
could use them: cash and freely sellable holdings; things that are yours but not reachable yet
(like vested shares not yet delivered); and things like property, retirement funds and fixed
deposits before maturity.

## Lucius

**Lucius** — the AI assistant built into the app. See [Lucius](../lucius/overview.md).

**Connector** — a link to an outside service: email, Telegram, a bank or broker, web search.
Each one is off until you add it. See the Connectors section of this help.

**Automation** — a job Lucius runs on a schedule without you, such as logging transactions from
your email. See [Automations](../lucius/automations.md).

**Always allow / Ask / Block** — the three settings every action Lucius can take has. The
approval mode only decides what Ask means. See [Permissions](../lucius/permissions.md).

**Confirm card** — the card Lucius shows before a change, with what will happen and buttons to
approve or reject it. See [Confirm cards](../lucius/confirm-cards.md).

**Checkpoint** — a snapshot the app takes before Lucius changes your data, so the change can be
undone.

**Skill** — saved instructions that teach Lucius how to do a specific task your way. See
[Skills](../lucius/skills.md).
