Start a paper portfolio

Three ways to start: with cash, with a copy of today's holdings, or with a followed portfolio. The fill settings are folded away, and the defaults are realistic, not perfect.

Why these choices

“Same as your investments” makes a paper result comparable with your real one. A copy of your holdings turns “what if I…” into a portfolio you can watch. Realistic fill defaults avoid the commonest complaint about paper trading elsewhere: fills that are too perfect.

The home currency is fixed after you create the portfolio, so returns stay comparable. Other currencies can still be held as cash.
