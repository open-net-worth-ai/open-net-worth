Paper Trading, v2

Paper Trading is a feature of the platform, not a mode the app switches into. A paper portfolio is a thing you own, like a watchlist or a followed portfolio. It carries one small Paper chip wherever it shows up, and everything else is the app's ordinary clean UI. All figures are made up.

  • The portfolio is marked, not the appNo toggle, no mode, no banner, no paper texture.
  • "Paper" is in the wordsPaper value, Paper order, Place paper buy, Paper buy filled.
  • The same screens as InvestmentsSummary, chart and holdings are the real components.
  • No borders except tablesGroups are made with fills and space.
  • Short copyA number and a short label. Comparisons live in the chart legend; detail lives one click away.
  • One accent, two placesThe chip and the main paper action button.
